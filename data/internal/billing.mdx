# Billing

Internal documentation for invoice exports, tax settings, and payment reconciliation. Topics here may require **admin** or **billing** group membership.

## Invoices

Invoice PDFs and structured exports can be generated for any closed billing period. Exports include line items, taxes, discounts, and currency metadata. Large exports may be delivered asynchronously via a download URL with time-limited access.

Configure tax behavior per region in the billing admin console. Changes apply to future invoices only unless you run an explicit recalculation job for open periods (requires elevated approval).

## Payment reconciliation

Match gateway settlements to internal ledger entries using the reconciliation report. Discrepancies are flagged when amounts or fee splits differ from expected values within configured tolerances.

Operators with **billing** scope can trigger manual retries for failed settlement batches. Actions are written to the audit trail with actor identity and timestamp.

## Credits and adjustments

Account credits may be issued for service incidents or contractual commitments. Each credit is tied to a reason code and approval workflow. Adjustments that affect recognized revenue require **admin** sign-off in regulated deployments.

## Data retention

Billing artifacts are retained according to your organization’s policy and applicable law. Requests for deletion or export should go through the privacy workflow; do not delete raw gateway identifiers without coordinating with finance and compliance.
